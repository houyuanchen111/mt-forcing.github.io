MT Forcing / Research overview

A good edit remembers
what came before.

Creating an image rarely ends with a single edit. We add ideas, refine details, and expect the subject, composition, and changes we already like to stay intact.

As a model repeatedly edits its own outputs, small errors can accumulate: textures degrade and content drifts. MT Forcing trains the model on its own multi-turn rollouts, with guidance from a single-turn teacher and real editing trajectories, to improve visual quality and consistency across successive edits.

02 / THE APPROACH

Single-turn clarity.
Multi-turn consistency.

MT Forcing addresses two challenges: maintaining visual quality while editing self-generated histories, and preserving content relationships across turns.

LEARNING ON SELF-ROLLOUTS

A single-turn teacher.
Guidance at every turn.

The student edits its own generated history, one turn at a time. The teacher starts from the original image and applies the accumulated instructions in a single pass, providing distribution-level guidance without carrying errors across turns.
